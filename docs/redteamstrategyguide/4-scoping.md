## Manage Inbound Demand

Use a single structured intake queue rather than reacting to whoever asks loudest. Capture the requesting organization, proposed target, desired outcome, business/security rationale, timing constraints, stakeholders, initial red-team assessment, and priority/status.

Some requests can be broadly visible when advance knowledge doesn't affect the measurement. Restrict others when awareness could cause defenders or system owners to prepare and invalidate the measurement.

## Prioritize, Don't Just Schedule

A long queue isn't a mandate to execute everything. Compare requests and proactively identified opportunities based on alignment with red-team purpose, potential organizational impact, realistic adversary relevance, strategic importance, the defenses being measured, and the unique value the red team can provide.
