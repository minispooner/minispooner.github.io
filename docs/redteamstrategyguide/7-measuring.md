## Demand as a Signal

Notes moved from Chapter 3:

Demand is a useful signal, not a measure of impact. A mature red team may generate plenty of strategically important operations itself; there isn't an ideal ratio of internally sourced versus externally requested operations.

If nobody requests red-team services, investigate whether employees understand the team's purpose and when to engage it, whether the team has demonstrated useful outcomes, whether previous engagements were difficult or unpleasant, and whether the organization's structure encourages inbound engagement. A reputation for policing rather than helping may also discourage requests.

"Indeed, as is true of any alternative-analysis unit, evaluating the Red Cell's eﬀectiveness in stirring minds or promoting out-of-the-box thinking is challenging because it can rarely be proven that any Red Cell product directly resulted in a new policy outcome or changed an oﬃcial's way of thinking about something." (Zenko, Red Team)
