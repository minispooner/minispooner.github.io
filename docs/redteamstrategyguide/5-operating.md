## Intro or ## Setting Clear Objectives
When I joined my first red team, I was exploiting things arbitrarily without an objective. I would find internal credentials and vulnerabilities and exploit them, looking for anything that could help the team and prove my value. Then my manager pulled me into a 1on1 and asked me if I was really in the right place. It wasn't a threat, it was a healthy, soul-searching question. What I had been doing was not red teaming, it was penetration testing without a clear objective. She offered to move me to a penetration team if that's what I wanted, otherwise, she recommended I pair up with another senior on the team who really understood red teaming and the adversary mindset. I spent the next year being mentored by that teammate and learning all I could about red teaming. Really understanding the purpose of red teaming and how it differs from other offensive security functions has played a huge role in my growth and impact.
